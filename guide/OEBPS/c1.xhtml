Hello, reader

pubre has a reading guide that carries you through the text at your pace. One key starts it.

Press Space · or tap ▶

“Start with one key,” said Professor Quill. “On a keyboard, press Space. On a phone, tap the round ▶ at the bottom.”

Try it now. The paragraph you’re on lifts off the page and a ring walks through it, word by word. Hold Space (or hold ▶) to read only while you hold; let go and the paragraph settles back where it was.

Listen to how it pauses. A comma, a short breath; a semicolon, a longer one. A full stop lands. Does a question rise? An exclamation jumps! And a thought that trails away…

Wren laughed. “It waits at the marks, the way I would.”

“That is the point,” said Professor Quill. “Reading has a rhythm. pubre keeps it, and you can make the pauses longer or shorter whenever you like.”
