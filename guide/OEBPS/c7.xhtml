Make it yours

Light, type and space are yours to set.

Paper, calm, quiet, focus, night · and palettes to tint them

Themes are a palette crossed with a mood, from bright paper to night black. Choose a font (there are monospace ones too), the size, line and letter spacing, justified or ragged text, and one page or two.

Your library keeps what you are reading on top and what is up next below. Each book has its own page, with its cover and the mood of every chapter. Finish one and pubre draws you a keepsake of it.

No account and no server: your books, notes and reading stay on this device, and pubre works offline.
