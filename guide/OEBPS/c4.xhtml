Who speaks, and what words mean

Read this little scene. pubre is already painting it.

“Is that a palimpsest?” asked Wren, holding the old page up to the lamp.

“It is,” said Professor Quill (who loved nothing more than being asked), “a page written over another page. Listen closely and you can almost hear the susurrus of the first ink.”

Wren turned it slowly. “It has a certain joie de vivre.”

“Everything old does,” said Professor Quill.

Names wear each character’s colour, and so do their spoken lines. A dotted word is rare: tap it for its meaning, looked up on your device. Words in another language are set apart; asides, like the one in the middle above, read a little quieter. Tap a name to see who they are so far.
