Who’s who

pubre reads each book once, on your device, and gets to know its people.

Who they are, where you met them, who they’re seen with

Open the menu and choose The cast: everyone you have met, drawn from their names, joined to the people they are seen with. It never reaches past your page, so there are no spoilers.

A new book takes a moment to read. While it does, a small chip at the top says so, and you see the people from the chapters behind you straight away.

“So I’m in the constellation?” asked Wren.

“Right in the middle,” said Professor Quill. “You speak the most.”
