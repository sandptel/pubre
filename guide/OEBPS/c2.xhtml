The Stage

The Stage is the reading guide: one paragraph at a time, lifted toward you, the rest of the page quietly behind it.

The ring walks the words · the capsule sets the pace

On a keyboard: ← → step a word, ↑ ↓ a paragraph, [ ] slower or faster, Esc back to the page.

On a phone: tap to pause, hold to glide. While holding, slide right to go faster, left to slow down and then go backwards, and up to lock it running.

“I like the ring,” said Wren. “Can it underline instead?”

“Nine ways,” said Professor Quill. “Open the quick panel, the Aa in the reading bar: move by word, phrase or sentence, choose a pointer, set how bright the read words, the current one and the ones ahead should be, change the text size, and how long it rests at each mark.”
